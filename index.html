Bring your own model. Get a complete webpage.

Stage 1 turns your notes into a full design brief — style, palette, type, layout, copy, a signature interactive module, and a lead-capture spec. Stage 2 turns that brief into one complete, self-contained webpage in a single HTML code block: a ready-to-save index.html file.

The brief is editable between stages. Rewrite anything you disagree with before you build.

  1. Point it at a model. For llama-server, use http://localhost:8080/v1 and leave the key blank.
  2. Write a few lines about the webpage you want. Detail is optional — gaps get filled and flagged.
  3. Run both stages, then copy, preview, or download your complete index.html webpage.

Nothing is sent anywhere except the endpoint you name. There is no backend here — this is one static file.
