Bring your own model. Get a whole page.

Stage 1 turns your notes into a full design brief — style, palette, type, layout, copy, a signature interactive module, and a lead-capture spec. Stage 2 builds that brief into one self-contained HTML file.

The brief is editable between stages. Rewrite anything you disagree with before you build.

  1. Point it at a model. For llama-server, use http://localhost:8080/v1 and leave the key blank.
  2. Write a few lines about the page. Detail is optional — gaps get filled and flagged.
  3. Run both stages, then copy, preview, or download the result.

Nothing is sent anywhere except the endpoint you name. There is no backend here — this is one static file.
